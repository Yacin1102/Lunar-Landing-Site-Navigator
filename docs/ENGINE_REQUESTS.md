# Engine requests from the web UI

Things the web app needs from the Python engine that do not exist yet. The UI rebuild (`docs/UI_PLAN.md`) may not edit `python/` or the contracts, so each need is written here with what the app does in the meantime. Nothing below is a contract change until it is approved.

| # | What | Why | Proposed form | What the app does until then |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A terrain skyline for a custom point | The brief asks for a sky view with a skyline at any typed latitude and longitude. The live `/api/skyline` (Contract 7) was removed on 2026-11-15, so only the 10 curated sites have one. | Either bring back `GET /api/skyline?lat=&lon=&mast_m=&az_step_deg=` returning the `Skyline` contract unchanged, or precompute skylines on a grid of the service region as `skylines/grid/{row}_{col}.json`. | A custom point shows the Sun and Earth directions, sizes and distances only. The smooth-sphere horizon is drawn dashed and labelled "no terrain data". No lit or blocked verdict, no visible fraction, no countdowns, no battery (handoff rule 5). |
| 2 | A wider terrain patch | The brief describes 257 × 257 samples over 20 km. The real patches are 241 × 241 at 20 m, 4.8 km across (`terrain/{id}.json`). At 4.8 km the ridges that cast the shadows are mostly outside the picture. | A second patch per site, for example `terrain/{id}_wide.bin` + `.json`, same contract, `n` = 257, `spacing_m` about 78, taken from the 20 m or 30 m map. A matching shade grid would be needed to light it. | The app draws the 4.8 km patch and reads `n`, `spacing_m` and `size_m` from the label. The scale bar shows the real size. |
| 3 | The sub-Moon point on the Earth | To turn the Earth texture so the right continents face the Moon, the app needs the Earth longitude and latitude directly under the Moon at each time. `ephem.bin` holds only positions in `MOON_ME`. | Two new Float32 columns in `ephem.bin`: `earth_sublon_deg`, `earth_sublat_deg`, from `spiceypy.subpnt("NEAR POINT/ELLIPSOID", "EARTH", et, "IAU_EARTH", "LT+S", "MOON")`, converted to degrees, longitude east-positive in -180 to 180. This changes `columns` in `ephem.json` and needs a contract version step. Longitude wraps, so the browser must interpolate it around the circle (or the engine stores its sine and cosine). | The Earth is drawn with a fixed orientation and a visible note "rotation approximate". Its phase is exact, because the phase needs only the Sun and Earth positions that already exist. |
| 4 | The sub-solar point on the Earth (optional) | Not needed for the phase. It would only allow a cross-check of the lit hemisphere against the texture. | Same call with `"SUN"` as observer. | Nothing; listed for completeness. |
